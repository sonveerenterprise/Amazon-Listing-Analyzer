INCREASE SALES - PROGRAMMES AND RULES (Amazon.in)
Source: Seller Central Help > Increase sales section (35 links). Condensed notes, read 17 Nov 2026.
Some links are only menu pages with no text of their own (Amazon Deals, Seller storefront, Amazon Prime, Seller Fulfilled Prime, Start selling, Additional resources).

==================================================
A. USEFUL FOR LISTING / LAUNCH GROWTH
==================================================

AMAZON VINE (G92T8UV339NZ98TN)
- Free units to trusted "Vine Voice" reviewers; builds reviews for new/slow ASINs.
- Requirements: Brand Registry (owner, representative or authorised reseller) AND FBA.
- Item must have fewer than 30 reviews, a buyable FBA "New" offer with stock in a fulfilment centre, never enrolled before, title + image + description, eligible browse node.
- Fee charged once per parent ASIN after the first Vine review is published; no fee if no review within 90 days.
- Not eligible: over INR 50,000, digital, precious jewellery, art, bullion, adult, heavy/bulky, multi-box.
- NOTE: an FBM-only seller cannot use Vine.

PRODUCT SAMPLING (GM2N7ZJQ59R7ESQP)
- Targeted buyers get your product for INR 1 through a claim code; Amazon notifies them via pages, emails, banners.
- Goal: early reviews, feedback, repeat buys, brand awareness. Claim codes hidden from non-target buyers.
- Works with ANY fulfilment: seller-fulfilled, Seller Fulfilled Prime, FBA, Seller Flex. Request via the Product Sampling programme form.
- The FBM alternative to Vine.

COUPONS (G3QLEV6W2QK84C57)
- Discount on one or a set of products; shown on the coupons page, search results, detail page, offers display and cart.
- Sub-topics: eligibility, budgets, targeting, errors, stacking.

PRICE DISCOUNTS (G7F8CQ4EJ5YA4272)
- Time-bound discount, 1-30 days, on a set number of units; can target Prime, all customers or business customers.
- Products with an MRP show a strike-through price.
- Eligibility: Professional account; product rating at least 3 stars; New condition; discount at least 5% below the 30-day lowest price AND at least 2% below current price; MRP needed to show savings.
- Seller-fulfilled offers also need: late shipment rate <= 4%, order defect rate <= 1%, pre-fulfilment cancellation <= 0.5%, disbursement reserve >= 7 days after delivery, FREE shipping.
- NOTE: zero-review / unrated products cannot use Price Discounts.

AMAZON PROMOTIONS (G60951)
- Manage Promotions: percentage/amount discounts, buy-X-get-Y free products, custom claim codes.
- A published promotion is binding: errors in setup create customer complaints or unplanned cost. Test rules carefully.
- Referral fee is charged on the price AFTER the promotion (free item: fee only on the paid item).

ALEXA FOR SHOPPING (GYYH9SLHSTHKT3CZ)
- Rufus + Alexa merged into one AI assistant; live for customers in India on app and website; no Prime needed.
- Customers use it for recommendations, comparisons, product updates, PRICE HISTORY (30/90/365 days of lowest featured-offer price), price alerts and Auto Buy, product details and "what customers say", orders.
- Product questions ("dimensions?", "machine washable?") are answered FROM THE DETAIL PAGE. Accurate attributes, bullets and description = accurate answers.
- Wrong answer? Fix the detail page first, then raise a help ticket with ASIN, screenshot of question + answer, what's wrong, expected answer, and proof (listing screenshot, manual, manufacturer site).
- NOTE: price history is visible to shoppers, so fake "discounts" off a raised price are exposed.

MANAGE YOUR EXPERIMENTS (GVP453K5XRBJS7Y9)
- A/B test title, images, bullets, description, A+ and Brand Story. Shoppers split 50/50 for the whole test. Experiments don't affect search rank.
- Requires Brand Registry brand owner AND a high-traffic ASIN (can mean several dozen orders a week). Low-traffic ASINs may not appear; drive traffic with ads first.
- One experiment per ASIN at a time. A+/Brand Story tests need published A+/Brand Story.

PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY EXPLORER (GNJ4YRTXWLMBY38U)
- Professional sellers only. Shows demand by "niche" (a group of related search terms and top products).
- Search niches by keyword, ASIN or category; pick the store (defaults to US first time).
- Use for: real search terms around a product, demand vs. competition, new product ideas.

SALE EVENTS (GTK7P2B845X27E4T, G5GW4WV64L2Z4KXK)
- Tips: stock up, spread stock across fulfilment centres (FBA), price competitively for the featured offer, use Lightning Deals and coupons (Amazon claims up to 3x sales), run Sponsored ads.
- Sale Event Planner (app + Seller Central): advance notice of events, recommended actions (deals, stock, Buy More Save More).

AMAZON ADS (G200663330)
- Sponsored Products / Brands / Display and Brand Stores are supported by the Advertiser Support team on the Amazon Ads site, not Seller Support.

WARRANTY DETAILS (G3GTEUDUFNFQBL69)
- Add warranty period, terms, how to claim, serviceable pin codes; shown on the detail page. Mostly for durable goods.

GIFT OPTIONS (G571)
- Seller-fulfilled sellers can enable gift wrap and gift messages per product (FBA handles this automatically).

AMAZON BAZAAR (GDJZZGNRXFYB9AG4, G6N49KFXCRFTLR5G, GCQFQQW5HW552SYN)
- Value store inside Amazon.in. Products at or below INR 500 (category-specific thresholds) with ZERO delivery charge, fulfilled by Easy Ship or self-ship, appear automatically. No separate listing.
- Check the thresholds table for your exact subcategory before relying on it.

FBA SUBSCRIBE & SAVE (G201620110)
- Recurring deliveries with a seller-funded base discount, extra tier for 3+ subscriptions. FBA only.

SAMRIDDHI (GJJ4GZUE4N6QBVUQ)
- Seller dashboard: performance tracker, monthly score card, speed, conversion levers, fee revision, selection recommendations; quick access to account manager.

BUSINESS PLANNER (GEANBS97BNTWSBJB, GRG94ATAYVWUZ5L4)
- Early-release tool: pick recommended goals from the homepage card and track them.

LIVE SHOPS (GPRDMH8SC6GZXYU9)
- Customers can video-call the seller during set slots for pre-purchase questions; configured in the Seller app.

AMAZON SMART COMMERCE SHOP BUILDER (GQTPHKTADT7G33HU)
- No-code brand website (formerly SmartBiz) that can import the Amazon.in catalogue; payments, shipping, marketing built in.

A+ CONTENT (G202102930)
- Same programme as file 07. Needs Professional account + Brand Representative or Reseller role.

==================================================
B. NOT RELEVANT FOR LOW-PRICE FOOD PRODUCTS
==================================================
- No Cost EMI, EMI with down payment, Exchange Offer, Buyback for self-ship, Cashback T&C: financing and trade-in programmes for higher-value goods.
- Digital Starter Kit: discounts on third-party services (accounting, GST, AWS) for new sellers.

CHECKLIST FOR THE SKILL (growth readiness)
==================================================
[ ] Fulfilment: FBA? (needed for Vine, Subscribe & Save) If FBM -> use Product Sampling for first reviews
[ ] Rating >= 3 stars before planning Price Discounts
[ ] Coupon running on new ASINs
[ ] Price <= INR 500 + free delivery -> Bazaar visibility (check subcategory threshold)
[ ] Account metrics within Price Discount limits (late shipment <= 4%, ODR <= 1%, cancel <= 0.5%)
[ ] Ask Alexa for Shopping the top buyer questions on the live page and check the answers
[ ] When traffic is high enough: A/B test title and main image with Manage Your Experiments
[ ] Use Product Opportunity Explorer niches for keyword research
