A+ CONTENT - RULES (Amazon.in)
Source: Seller Central Help > A+ Content guide (GLG4RQK2Y2RJADU4). Condensed notes, read 17 Nov 2026.

WHAT
- Rich content in the product description section, for brand owners (Brand Registry) and authorised resellers.
- Free. Premium A+ has no extra eligibility criteria.
- Benefits: better conversion and discoverability, fewer questions and negative reviews, stronger brand.

THREE TYPES
- Basic A+: up to 5 modules, 14 module types, images 970 x 300, comparison chart, no video/hotspots.
- Premium A+: up to 7 modules, 19 module types, images 1464 x 600, video, hotspots, carousels, Q&A modules, enhanced comparison charts.
- Brand Story ("From the brand"): 1 module, up to 19 carousel cards, links to products and Brand Store. Works alongside Basic or Premium.

TECH
- .jpg .bmp .png, RGB only (no CMYK), under 2 MB each, 72 dpi+.
- No GIFs, HTML tags, watermarks, QR codes, hyperlinks/redirects.
- Language must match the content language.
- Brand name must exactly match Brand Registry (case-sensitive). ASINs must be in your brand.

PROCESS
- A+ Content Manager > Start creating > choose modules > add text + images (with ALT TEXT for every image) > preview desktop + mobile > apply to ASINs > submit.
- Review up to 7 business days; live within 24 h of approval. Max 20 pending submissions.
- Bulk apply: up to 10,000 ASINs per upload.

DO
- Unique content (don't repeat the image gallery)
- Features and benefits, not promo language
- Clear alt text on all images
- Max 3 sentences per paragraph
- Consistent terms, proper header capitals, serial commas, sparing bold/italic
- Spell out numbers under 10
- Readable text, clear hierarchy, mobile-friendly
- Answer common customer questions
- Comparison charts for SAME-brand products

DON'T
- Time-sensitive references, promo language or pricing
- Competitor comparisons
- External links or contact info
- Warranty/guarantee info, shipping details
- QR codes, personal info, watermarks, animated images
- Blurry images or tiny text; images duplicated from the gallery

IF IT DOESN'T SHOW
- Check status Approved > ASIN under Applied ASINs > language > wait 24 h > contact support.
- If rejected: read rejection reasons, fix, resubmit.

CHECKLIST FOR THE SKILL
[ ] A+ present (Premium if possible: 7 modules)
[ ] Brand Story present
[ ] Alt text on every image
[ ] No duplicate modules / gallery repeats
[ ] Comparison chart linking own ASINs (e.g. pack sizes or formats)
[ ] FAQ / Q&A module answering top buyer questions
[ ] No pricing, promo, competitor names, guarantees, links
