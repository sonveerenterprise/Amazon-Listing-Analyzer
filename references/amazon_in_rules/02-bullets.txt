BULLET POINTS - RULES (Amazon.in)
Source: Seller Central Help > Product bullet point requirements (GX5L8BF8GLMML6CX). Condensed notes, read 17 Nov 2026.

WRITING GUIDELINES
- At least 3 bullets. Character limit depends on product type.
- Start with a capital letter.
- Write as a sentence fragment; NO full stop at the end. Use semicolons to separate phrases inside one bullet.
- Numbers one to nine written as words (except names, model numbers, measurements).
- Space between number and unit: "60 ml", not "60ml".
- Structure: short header + colon + description ("Cotton fabric: Made from 100% cotton").
- Focus on features, benefits and customer needs. No brand marketing stories.
- Keep data consistent across variants. Don't duplicate what's already in title, description or product overview.
- Avoid subjective, performance or comparison claims unless verifiable on the packaging. No unproven awards or survey results.
- Don't point to other products or compare with competitor brands.

PROHIBITED (bullet may be removed or rewritten by Amazon)
- Special characters: ™ ® € … † ‡ ¢ £ ¥ © ± ~ â
- Emojis
- Placeholder text: NA, N/A, TBD, "to be decided", "copy pending" etc.
- ASINs
- Claims: eco-friendly / environmentally friendly, anti-microbial, anti-bacterial, made from/contains bamboo or soy (see General listing restrictions)
- Guarantees: "full refund", "send it back if not satisfied", unconditional guarantees
- External info: company info, website links, contact details
- Same information repeated across bullets (each bullet must be unique)
- ALL CAPS ("MACHINE WASHABLE")
- Abbreviations: qty, pkg, w/, approx.
- Info that has its own field (fabric, care instructions, country of origin)

HOW TO EDIT
- Single product: Manage Products > Edit > Product Details > Bullet Point > Save.
- Bulk: category inventory file template (Add products > Spreadsheet).

CHECKLIST FOR THE SKILL
[ ] 3+ bullets (5 used in practice)
[ ] Each starts with "Header: description"
[ ] No end punctuation
[ ] No ALL CAPS headers
[ ] No repeated point across bullets
[ ] No banned characters, emojis, ASINs, links, contact info
[ ] No guarantees, no competitor comparisons, no unverifiable claims
[ ] "60 ml" spacing; numbers 1-9 as words where not a measurement
[ ] Nothing that belongs in a dedicated field (origin, care, fabric)
