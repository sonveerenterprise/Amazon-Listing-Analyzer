SHOPPABLE VIDEO - RULES (Amazon.in)
Source: Seller Central Help > Shoppable video guide (GUZBYWTTVULY6C9Y). Condensed notes, read 17 Nov 2026.

WHY
- Amazon's own data: ASINs that added a video saw about 24% more sales on average than ASINs without one (Jan-Jun 2026).
- Builds confidence, shows size/use, reduces returns.

VIDEO TYPES
- Product overview (features, real-life use, size)
- Unboxing (what's in the box, scale)
- How-to / product operation (multiple use cases)
- Setup, Troubleshooting, Brand story, Other

TECH SPECS
- MOV or MP4, max 5 GB, up to 1080p.
- 16:9 horizontal or 9:16 vertical.
- Duration 1-12 minutes.
- Thumbnail JPEG/PNG/GIF/BMP under 5 MB.
- Each video linked to at least one ASIN. No cross-marketplace sharing (except US-Canada).
- Upload eligibility: seller account active more than 3 months.

ALLOWED
- Features and benefits, product in use, size/scale, setup steps, answering common customer questions, good lighting and clear audio, engaging thumbnail.

NOT ALLOWED
- External website links, promotional calls to action, promotional content
- Unlicensed music/artwork, visible third-party branding
- Misleading claims or wrong information
- Blurry/low-quality video or thumbnails
- AI-generated photorealistic people: tag with "contains-synthetic-performer" metadata.

UPLOAD
- Upload and Manage Videos > Upload video(s): title, ASINs, language, thumbnail > Submit.
- Or through Image Manager.

CHECKLIST FOR THE SKILL
[ ] Listing has at least 1 video
[ ] Shows product in use and answers top buyer questions
[ ] 1080p, 16:9 or 9:16, 1-12 min
[ ] No links, calls to action, promo, unlicensed music, false claims
