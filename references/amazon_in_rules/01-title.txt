PRODUCT TITLE - RULES (Amazon.in)
Source: Seller Central Help > Product title requirements and guidelines (GYTR6SYGFA5E3EQC). Condensed notes, read 17 Nov 2026.

HARD REQUIREMENTS (a listing can be blocked, auto-corrected or hidden from search)
- Max 75 characters including spaces. Applies to all product types except media.
- No promotional phrases (e.g. "free shipping", "100% quality guaranteed").
- Banned characters: ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦
- ~ # < > * allowed only in a real context (model number "Style #4301", measurement "<10 lb"). Decorative use of symbols is not allowed.
- Must contain at least the minimum info that identifies the product (brand + product type).
- No restricted phrases (example given: "FSA/HSA eligible").
- No word more than twice. Prepositions, articles and conjunctions are exempt. The brand name also counts toward the two-use limit.

BEST PRACTICES
- Order: Brand > flavour/style > product type > key attribute > colour > size or pack count > model number. Most important info first.
- Keep it short and scannable; mobile cuts long titles.
- No repeated words or information.
- No decorative symbols (stars etc.). Allowed punctuation: - / , & .  No non-language characters like Æ Š Œ.
- Variations: size/colour go in the child title, not the parent title.
- Use numerals ("2-Pack"), not words ("Two-Pack").
- No subjective words ("Hot item", "Best seller").
- No ALL CAPS and no all lowercase. Capitalise each word except prepositions, conjunctions and articles.
- Measurements may be abbreviated (cm, oz, in, kg).
- Model numbers recommended in some categories (check category style guide).

ITEM HIGHLIGHTS (extra field under the title)
- Unlocked once the title is within 75 characters.
- About 125 extra characters for details like material or use case.
- Write as comma-separated phrases, not sentences.
- Shown under the title in search results and on the detail page.
- Put the most important variating attributes in the title; overflow goes to item highlights.

PROCESSING AND TROUBLESHOOTING
- Changes are accepted immediately but take 24-48 hours to show. No need to resubmit or open a case in that window. After 48 hours: resubmit or contact support.
- No brand: enter "generic" in Brand name; never put a brand in the title.
- Brand names with banned characters go in the Brand name field only (exempt there).
- Parent title is what shows on the detail page in Clothing, Accessories & Luggage, Sporting Goods and Beauty. Edit the parent title in those categories.
- Suppressed/blocked title: Manage All Inventory > "Search suppressed and inactive listings", fix the violation, resubmit; Amazon re-checks automatically.

CHECKLIST FOR THE SKILL
[ ] Length <= 75
[ ] No banned characters / decorative symbols
[ ] No promo or subjective words
[ ] No word used 3+ times (incl. brand)
[ ] Starts with brand, then product type and key attribute
[ ] Numerals, not number words
[ ] Title case, not ALL CAPS
[ ] Item highlights filled, comma-separated phrases, <= ~125 chars
